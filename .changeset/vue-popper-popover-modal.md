Add `Popper`, `Popover` and `Modal`, mirroring `@okkly/react`'s components of the same names. `Popper` positions a slot against an anchor with `@popperjs/core`; `Popover` adds Escape, click-outside, a surface and a scale+fade transition; `Modal` is the portal/backdrop/focus-trap/scroll-lock primitive both dialogs and drawers are built on. They are built on the composables now published as `@okkly/vue-composables`.

Add `FileUpload`, a drop zone for selecting files with drag & drop, per-file validation and a selection list, mirroring `@okkly/react`'s `FileUpload`. `@okkly/vue-composables` gains the headless `useFileUpload` composable it's built on, the Vue port of `@okkly/react-hooks`'s `useFileUpload`.

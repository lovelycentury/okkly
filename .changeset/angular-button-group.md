Add `OkklyButtonGroup` (`okkly-button-group`), a split button mirroring `@okkly/react`'s `<ButtonGroup>`, composed from `button[okklyButtonGroupAction]` (with an optional `okklyButtonGroupIcon`) and `button[okklyButtonGroupMenuItem]` entries: two variants, six tones, a chevron menu that closes on pick, Escape and outside click, and a `disabled` state.

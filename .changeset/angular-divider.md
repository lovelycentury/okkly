Add `OkklyDivider` (`okkly-divider`), a hairline separator for lists, stacks and toolbars, mirroring `@okkly/react`'s `<Divider>`: horizontal or vertical, `inset`/`middle` variants, `flexItem`, and an optional label tagged `okklyDividerLabel` aligned left, center or right.

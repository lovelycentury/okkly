Add `OkklySelect` (`okkly-select`), a closed-list field with single and multi-select, grouping, clearing, loading and a native form fallback via `name`. Also adds the `OkklyOption*` primitives (`OkklyOptionScope`, `OkklyOptionRow`, `OkklyOptionLabel`, `OkklyOptionDescription`, `OkklyOptionBody`, `OkklyOptionCheck`, `OkklyHighlightMatch`) it's built from, shared with a future `OkklyAutocomplete`.

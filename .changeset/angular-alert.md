Add `OkklyAlert` (`okkly-alert`), an inline `role="alert"` banner mirroring `@okkly/react`'s `<Alert>`: five severities, three variants, a title, a severity icon that can be swapped (`okklyAlertIcon`) or hidden, an `okklyAlertAction` slot, and a `closable` dismiss button that emits `close`.
